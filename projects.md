I have worked on many projects in my career where I was able to learn and develop my skills. 

Skip has designed and implemented secure on-premises and cloud-based environments supporting development, testing, staging, production, and continuity-of-operations requirements. Key efforts include VMware-based development environments; SQL Server Always On Availability Group implementations; AWS cloud migrations and security-stack designs; and development-pipeline environments incorporating tools such as Azure DevOps, OpenShift, Git, database platforms, web services, and collaboration tools. He has also delivered custom web applications, SharePoint solutions, reporting platforms, database systems, data-migration processes, and technical documentation supporting complex government programs.

## Mission Assurance Decision Support System (MADSS)
MADDS is an application designed to reduce potential risks of degraded or failed communications missions by
developing techniques and implementing tools to fight cyber or kinetic attacks. The application informs
commanders of mission readiness, where their risks are, and what can be done to remediate them.


## Naval Systems Engineering Research Center (NSERC)
This was a larger project I supported for years that provides Research, Development, Test, and Evaluation (RDT&E) infrastructure and engineering tools to DoD Programs to meet their specific engineering and development requirements. This infrastructure operated across two classifications and offered many tools for Software development teams (Atlassian suite, TFS, Azure DevOps, Gitlab, etc.), Customized tracking capabilities within SharePoint, tools for Systems Engineers (DOORS, Cameo, MATLAB, MagicDrAW, Rhapsody, Innoslate, etc.).

## Electronic Warare On Boaard Trainer (EWOBT)
This was a project I worked very early in my career (1987-1989) where our team used a custom application called the Computer Aided Training System (CATS) to design, compile, and deliver computer based training to the U.S. Navy designed for the Electronic Warfare (EW) Rate.
